# OperatorService

**Source:** [[OperatorService.java](../../src/main/java/com/ple/visur/OperatorService.java)]
**Date updated:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

The OperatorService manages the lifecycle and registration of editing operators. It provides methods to register, retrieve, and execute operators. The service is responsible for mapping operator names to their implementations and executing them when triggered by the command execution service.[^1]

## Key Takeaways

- Manages operator lifecycle and registration
- Maps operator names to implementations
- Executes operators when triggered
